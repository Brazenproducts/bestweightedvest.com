## Content Overview

### Main Topics Covered
1. **Why Fall Maintenance Matters** — cascading winter damage prevention
   - Salt penetration and seam corrosion
   - Mold colonization in cold/wet conditions
   - UV damage from low winter sun
   - Freeze-thaw cycle damage at stress points

2. **Material-Specific Prep**
   - Tactical Cordura (polyurethane backing) — seam sealer focus
   - Neoprene — protectant spray + moisture control
   - Canvas — hydrophobic wax treatment
   - Heavy polyester (Smittybilt G.E.A.R.) — moisture management

3. **Month-by-Month Timeline**
   - Week 1 (Sept 1-7): Deep clean
   - Week 2-3 (Sept 8-21): Protective coatings
   - Week 4 (Sept 22-30): Drain/dry checks
   - Monthly checks (Oct-Dec): 10-minute inspections

4. **Regional Strategies**
   - High-salt regions: Seam sealer mandatory, ventilation critical
   - Wet-climate zones: Mold prevention focus, dehumidifier key
   - Dry-cold regions: UV protection, thermal cycle management

5. **Real-World Schedule**
   - Total setup time: 2-3 hours in fall
   - Ongoing maintenance: 10-20 minutes/month
   - Spring reset: 2 hours
   - Cost: $50-80 for complete seasonal kit

6. **Troubleshooting FAQ**
