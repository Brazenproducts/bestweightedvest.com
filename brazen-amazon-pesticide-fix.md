## Why This Fix Works

**Amazon's Rule:** Claims that a product prevents, destroys, repels, or mitigates pests (including mold) classify it as a pesticide device under EPA/FIFRA rules.

**Our Fix:**
- ✅ Describe material properties (silicone's inertness, non-porous surface)
- ✅ Explain performance benefits (resists mineral buildup, maintains spray)
- ✅ Focus on durability in wet environments
- ❌ No claims about preventing/resisting mold or mildew
- ❌ No "mold-proof", "anti-mold", "mold resistant" language

This keeps the listing compliant while still communicating the product's value.
