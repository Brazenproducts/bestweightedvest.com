## Priority 1: Google Business Profile (Highest Value)

**URL:** https://business.google.com  
**Why:** Google-owned, instant indexing boost, shows in Maps/Local Pack  
**Verification:** Phone or postcard (use phone for faster approval)  
**Service Area Business:** YES (select "I deliver goods to customers")  
**Time per site:** 15 minutes  

**Steps:**
1. Go to business.google.com
2. Click "Add your business to Google"
3. Enter business name (exact match to domain)
4. Choose category: "Industrial Equipment Supplier" or "Packaging Supply Store"
5. Select "Yes, I deliver goods to customers" (skip physical location)
6. Add service area: Los Angeles County (or "United States")
7. Add phone number for verification
8. Verify via automated call
9. Add website URL, hours, description

## Priority 2: ThomasNet (B2B Authority)

**URL:** https://business.thomasnet.com  
**Why:** #1 B2B industrial directory, huge domain authority (DA 90+)  
**Verification:** Email  
**Time per site:** 12 minutes  

**Steps:**
1. Go to business.thomasnet.com
2. Click "Add Your Company"
3. Company name, address, phone, website
4. Select industry categories (Packaging, Material Handling, Logistics)
5. Add product/service descriptions (use site meta descriptions)
6. Upload logo (use generic product image if no logo)
7. Verify email
8. Publish profile
